Gen 3 SID Finder

Find possible SIDs for Generation III Pokémon games on Nintendo Switch using a TID and G7TID.

Where do I find these numbers?

The 5-digit TID is shown in the original GBA game. The 6-digit G7TID is visible after transferring a Pokémon to a mainline title such as Pokémon Sword/Shield, Scarlet/Violet, or Legends: Z-A.
